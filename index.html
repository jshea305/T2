Who We Are

Take 2: Dan Reynolds & Jeff Shea

We are Take 2, a versatile and musically experienced duo that can cover a wide variety of musical styles and selections. We offer our clients the opportunity to choose to hire Take 2 as either our classical flute/acoustic guitar duo or our saxophone/flute and jazz guitar duo.

Performance Styles & Ensembles

Classical Flute / Acoustic Guitar Duo

Take 2 specializes in performing music for wedding ceremonies as well as receptions, dinners, corporate events, and private parties. We perform acoustically for small intimate settings or carefully amplified for outdoor ceremonies or larger rooms. We are a portable, versatile, and affordable duo covering a wide variety of musical styles.

Saxophone/Flute & Jazz Guitar Duo

Available for your wedding cocktail hour, receptions, dinners, corporate events, and private parties. We perform light jazz, ballads, standards, and contemporary selections to create an elegant, artistic, and memorable ambiance. We also feature an extensive repertoire of tasteful seasonal holiday music.

Eclectic Classical & Jazz Combination

If your event lends itself toward diverse musical genres, choose an eclectic blend. We can provide majestic classical music for religious services, grace for garden ceremonies, and contemporary or traditional jazz standards for cocktail hour.

Clarinet/Flute & Guitar Duo (Jewish Music)

We feature a collection of over 200 Jewish songs (Klezmer, Israeli, Yiddish, Sephardic, Holiday, and Hassidic) for Jewish weddings, bar/bat mitzvahs, receptions, or special events—most commonly performed with clarinet and acoustic guitar.

Service Area: Take 2 is based in Rhode Island and is happy to travel to Massachusetts or Connecticut. Longer distances are booked based on availability.
