Who We Are

Take 2: Dan Reynolds & Jeff Shea

Welcome to Take 2—a versatile musical duo featuring Dan Reynolds and Jeff Shea. We bring sophisticated, live acoustic and jazz arrangements to weddings, corporate galas, and private events across New England. From elegant classical ceremony music to smooth cocktail-hour jazz, we tailor our instrumentation to create the perfect atmosphere for your celebration.

Performance Styles & Ensembles

Classical Flute & Acoustic Guitar

Elevate your wedding ceremony, formal dinner, or intimate reception with timeless classical music. We perform completely acoustically for close-knit settings or utilize professional, low-profile amplification for outdoor venues and larger halls. Perfectly portable, flexible, and tailored to your ideal setlist.

Saxophone/Flute & Jazz Guitar

Set an inviting, sophisticated mood for your cocktail hour, corporate dinner, or private party. Our jazz repertoire covers smooth ballads, American Songbook standards, pop arrangements, and a full catalog of tasteful holiday favorites designed to keep the ambiance refined and memorable.

Eclectic Classical & Jazz Blend

Can’t decide on just one style? Combine genres for a seamless event flow. Experience majestic classical arrangements during your ceremony, followed by an effortless transition into smooth, contemporary jazz standards for cocktail hour and dinner.

Clarinet/Flute & Guitar (Jewish Ceremonies & Events)

We offer an extensive repertoire of over 200 traditional and modern Jewish selections—including Klezmer, Israeli, Yiddish, Sephardic, and Hassidic songs. Arranged primarily for clarinet and acoustic guitar, this ensemble is ideal for Jewish wedding ceremonies, Bar/Bat Mitzvahs, and milestone celebrations.

Service Area: Based in Rhode Island, Take 2 regularly performs throughout RI, Massachusetts, and Connecticut. Extended travel across New England is available upon request.
